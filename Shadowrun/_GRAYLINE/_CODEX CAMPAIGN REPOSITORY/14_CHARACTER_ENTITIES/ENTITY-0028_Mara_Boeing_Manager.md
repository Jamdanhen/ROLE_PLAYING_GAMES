## Approved Fact

Mara is a confirmed Boeing employee at manager level.

Mara lost the Kestrel-owned crate at the Grayline docks when the Burnouts stole it.

After the crate was stolen, Mara hired Blackjack to find someone to recover it.

Blackjack hired the player characters.

The player characters handed the crate off to Toggle and Skittles.

Toggle and Skittles were Blackjack associates working a separate handoff contract for Blackjack.

Toggle and Skittles returned the crate to Mara.

Boeing took both Mara and the crate after the return.

Graham Voss was Mara's supervisor and may or may not have known about the situation.

## Boundary
