Player-Facing Summary / Session 006 / All In

Session 006 - All In

The night opened with the Siberian Wolf preparing for a larger-than-normal Poker Night. The Chin called in extra help, Tiny worked security, and the Wolf filled with gamblers, regulars, and outside attention.

The Marrow Meeting

The runners met the Bourgeoisie at The Marrow. Switch, Kilmer, and The Chin each received 8,000¥. SUV ownership paperwork was transferred, along with a contact phone and Oleg's information. Graham was no longer in runner custody and had moved through the Bourgeoisie arrangement.

Braddock Pressure

Switch received pressure from Lieutenant Braddock, who sent a surveillance photo and the message:

"I know. Boeing doesn't. Yet..."

Braddock tried to use Switch's past and old badge identity against him, but Switch rejected him.

Poker Night

Back at the Wolf, Poker Night became crowded and strange. Faust drew Kilmer into the table. Ellis Rouke watched Kilmer too closely. Liang "Mouse" Zhen drew attention after Kilmer noticed his neck tattoo. Velvet Jones arrived with an entourage, dominated the room, asked repeatedly about Kilmer, and later helped cover the team's back-office absence.

Technical Review

In the back-office technical review, Toggle, Skittles, Switch, Kilmer, and The Chin examined the hard drive and related pursuit evidence. They found signs of telemetry, traffic tracing, camera reconstruction, movement analysis, and organized tracking. Toggle also recovered a tracker from the motorcycle, helping explain how the trail had been reacquired.

Mouse And Ellis

Switch identified Mouse's tattoo as Dragon, marking him as a Triad lieutenant. Mouse surrendered two concealed short blades, dropped the broken-English act, and spoke fluently. He admitted he knew who The Chin was, knew the Wolf was associated with Grayline Dogs, and was gathering information. He has not explained his full purpose.

Ellis's freight-worker cover came apart. Nails Donovan recognized that Ellis was not freight, not shipping, and not logistics. Ellis refused to cooperate, tried to leave, and was contained by Tiny and Frost. At the end of the session, Ellis remained contained and refusing cooperation.

Velvet's Hook

Velvet Jones called out to The Chin and hinted at future work:

"Baby, I got a job for you. Someone needs moved. Sometimes gravity don't pull hard enough and you gotta pull a person in a direction. I got somebody making moves that shouldn't be making them. We'll talk tomorrow. Looks like you got your hands full."

End State

  • Mouse is cooperative but not fully honest.
  • Ellis is contained and resisting.
  • Kilmer is down 10,000¥ from gambling.
  • Blackjack is still recovering.
  • Boeing pressure is still looming.
  • Multiple threads are waiting to break open.
