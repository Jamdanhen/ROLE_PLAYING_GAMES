## Result

### May 24, 2057 - Session 001 Begins

The crew attacks the Burnout-held warehouse, eliminates six Burnouts, recovers the crate intact, and leaves after a grenade causes the stored motorcycles and warehouse environment to catch fire.

### May 24-27, 2057 - Session 002

The team keeps the crate unopened, negotiates with Blackjack, receives increased payment, delivers the crate through Toggle and Skittles, returns to the Siberian Wolf, and learns Lone Star is investigating the warehouse fire. Blackjack becomes missing/non-responsive.

### May 31, 2057 - Session 003

Kestrel pressure continues. Blackjack survives interrogation and does not betray the runners. Hubcap recovers the hard drive. The team identifies Graham Voss as a likely target and extracts him from Boeing-linked surveillance.

### June 1, 2057 - Session 004

Graham and the hard drive are in runner possession. Boeing reconstruction is active. The motorcycle tracer is active. Ares begins independent convergence. Grayline pressure and instability increase.

### June 1, 2057 - Session 005

The runners accept the Bourgeoisie's terms for Graham, leave the chateau under corporate pressure, identify Boeing's surveillance and motorcycle trace behavior, and process street-level fallout around Donnie, Blackjack, Hubcap, Rezak / "Razor", and Snacks.

## Continuity Result

- The query correctly includes Sessions 001 through 005.
- The query correctly excludes Session 000.
- Sessions 004 and 005 both fall on June 1, 2057 but are separated by time window.
- Session 002 spans multiple in-game dates and should be included when any part of its range overlaps the query range.
