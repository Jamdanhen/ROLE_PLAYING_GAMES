# Faust Pressure Theory

Type:
GM-facing campaign theory / pressure model

Visibility:
GM-facing

Status:
Active theory, versioned

Theory version:
THEORY-FAUST-0001

## Theory

Faust operates as a catalyst and manipulator of pressure.

He does not need to create monsters. People are usually sufficient.

He identifies existing cracks, emotions, conflicts, and grievances, then encourages them to widen.

## What Faust Encourages

- Retaliation over restraint.
- Pride over practicality.
- Certainty over reflection.
- Suspicion over trust.
- Immediate gain over long-term stability.
- Ambition over caution.
- Escalation over compromise.

## What Faust Opposes

- Clarity.
- Self-reflection.
- Emotional discipline.
- Reconciliation.
- Successful mediation.
- Informed restraint.
- Cooperative solutions.
- Decisions made without emotional pressure.

## Triad / Tsar Conflict

The Triad / Tsar conflict is genuine and exists independently of Faust.

Faust benefits when compromise fails, escalation succeeds, and each side interprets the other in the worst possible way.

## Artifact Arc Connection

GM-only working theory:

The Chinese artifact may be dangerous to Faust's interests because it could reduce his ability to influence individuals who properly employ its associated ritual tradition.

The artifact is framed as a countermeasure, not a weapon, prison, or destroyer.

## Possible Weakness

No confirmed weakness exists.

Current working theory:
Faust's influence may become less effective when individuals achieve clarity, emotional pressure decreases, decisions are made deliberately, or manipulation is recognized.
