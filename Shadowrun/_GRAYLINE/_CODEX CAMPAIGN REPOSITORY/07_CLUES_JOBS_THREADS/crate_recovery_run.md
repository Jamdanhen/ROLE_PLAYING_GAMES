# Crate Recovery Run

Sources:
- 02_SESSIONS/SESSION_001.MD
- 02_SESSIONS/SESSION_002.MD

## Type
Job / run

## Status
Completed externally.

## Confirmed Facts
- In Session 1, Burnouts stole cargo more dangerous than weapons.
- The stolen cargo was a Kestrel-owned crate.
- Mara, a Boeing manager-level employee, lost the crate at the Grayline docks when the Burnouts stole it.
- Mara hired Blackjack to find someone to recover the crate.
- Blackjack hired the player characters.
- Crate was dragged from docks via chained van.
- Box truck is staged for later extraction.
- Stealth operation collapses after commlink feedback alerts Burnouts.
- Team adapts into multi-angle assault: The Chin exposed/elevated, Switch front breach, Kilmer overwatch.
- Six Burnout gang members are eliminated.
- Crate is located, secured, loaded into box truck with lift assist, and recovered intact.
- Team exits in box truck; Burnout patrol passes with no detection.
- Session 1 objective status: crate recovered, origin partially identified by Kestrel markings, crate intact.
- Crate begins Session 2 secured in team possession and stored in a box truck.
- Team contacts Blackjack for drop-off location.
- Payment increases to 4000 nuyen per runner.
- Crate must remain untampered and delivery must occur that night.
- Team declines to open the crate.
- The player characters handed the crate off to Toggle and Skittles.
- Toggle and Skittles were Blackjack associates working a separate handoff contract for Blackjack.
- Toggle and Skittles returned the crate to Mara.
- Crate is delivered through Toggle's drone system.
- Boeing took both Mara and the crate after the return.
- Payment is delivered via courier drop.
- Run is completed clean externally.

## Fragile Consequence
- Crate condition may raise questions upstream.
- Kestrel owns the crate and may become a future exposure risk if it learns Boeing handled the recovery.
