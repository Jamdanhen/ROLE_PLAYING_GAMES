## Velvet's Problem

Markus Vail is moving into Velvet's influence territory.

The problem is not just the club.

The problem is that Vail has collected compromising material on high-profile people and is beginning to use that archive as leverage.

Vail's power is proof.

## Locked Job Principle

The club remains the public-facing power base.

The archive is off-site.

The job is not:
Hit the club.

The job is:
Find and destroy the secret storage archive that lets Vail compromise high-profile people.

## Archive Location

Location:

Hidden contents:

## Why The Archive Is Not At The Club

- If Vail's club is raided, searched, burned, robbed, or watched, the archive is exposed.
- Anyone looking for leverage would check the club first.
- Club offices are noisy, busy, staff-accessible, and full of witnesses.
- Club security protects the business, not the secret.
- Vail is too careful to keep his most valuable blackmail material where angry rivals would naturally look.

## Velvet's Objective

Primary:
Destroy or remove Vail's off-site archive.

Secondary:
Leave the club untouched.

Optional:
Bring Velvet proof.

Proof options:
- Specific ledger cover.
- Burned index page.
- Vail's coded client key.
- Photograph of the archive destroyed.
- One sealed envelope Velvet names in advance.
- Specific black road case marked with an event company logo.

Velvet preference:
- Physical destruction.
- Minimal public noise.
- No bodies if avoidable.
- No attention leading back to Velvet.
- No direct hit on the club.

## Heat Logic

If done clean:
- It looks like a storage burglary.
- Vail may not report it because the stolen or destroyed items are incriminating.
- Police response is minimal.
- The storage facility wants quiet resolution.
- High-profile compromised people do not want attention.
- Vail is hurt but cannot openly explain why.

If done loud:
- Storage security responds.
- Vail's private muscle responds later.
- Some compromised people panic.
- Rumors spread through nightlife.
- Heat rises, but not instantly from corporations or Lone Star unless the runners leave bodies, fire, or obvious evidence.
