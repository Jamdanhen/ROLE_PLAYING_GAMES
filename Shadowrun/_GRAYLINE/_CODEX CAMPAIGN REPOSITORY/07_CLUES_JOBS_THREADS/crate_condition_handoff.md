Sources:
- 02_SESSIONS/SESSION_001.MD
- 02_SESSIONS/SESSION_002.MD

## Type
Item / thread

## Confirmed Facts
- The crate belonged to Kestrel.
- Mara, a Boeing manager-level employee, lost the crate at the Grayline docks when the Burnouts stole it.
- In Session 1, crate is recovered intact.
- Origin is partially identified through Kestrel markings.
- Crate is banged up, scratched, and visibly worn from Burnouts gang handling.
- No heat signatures are detected.
- No residue is detected.
- External damage shows scrapes and impact wear consistent with rough transport.
- Crate remains unopened before delivery.
- The player characters handed the crate off to Toggle and Skittles.
- Toggle and Skittles were Blackjack associates working a separate handoff contract for Blackjack.
- Toggle and Skittles returned the crate to Mara.
- Crate is unloaded via drone system.
- Crate is moved to a jet ski-sized drone craft and transported across the river.
- Boeing took both Mara and the crate after the return.

## Chain Of Custody
- Kestrel owned the crate.
- Burnouts stole it from the Grayline docks while Mara was responsible for it.
- Mara hired Blackjack to arrange recovery.
- Blackjack hired the player characters.
- The player characters recovered the crate and handed it off to Toggle and Skittles.
- Toggle and Skittles were Blackjack associates on a separate handoff contract.
- Toggle and Skittles returned the crate to Mara.
- Boeing took custody of Mara and the recovered crate.
