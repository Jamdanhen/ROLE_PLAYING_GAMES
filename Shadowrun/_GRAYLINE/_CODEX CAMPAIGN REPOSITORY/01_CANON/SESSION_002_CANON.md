- Primary locations: North Side, South Side, Gray Line District, Pier 12, Pier 15, Rusty Spade, Siberian Wolf

## Confirmed Facts
- The crate begins Session 2 secured in team possession and stored in a box truck.
- The crate is banged up, scratched, and visibly worn from Burnouts gang handling.
- The team contacts Blackjack for a drop-off location.
- Blackjack provides Pier 12 on the North Side as the initial delivery location.
- The team crosses to South Side with no incident and holds under a bridge with the truck concealed and the crate unopened.
- The Chin and Kilmer inspect the crate with enhanced vision and magnification.
- No heat signatures or residue are detected.
- Scrapes and impact wear are consistent with rough transport.
- Switch reports to Blackjack that the crate may be higher value.
- Blackjack requests a face-to-face meeting at Rusty Spade.
- At Rusty Spade, Blackjack increases payment to 4000 nuyen per runner.
- Blackjack requires the crate to remain untampered and delivered that night.
- Mara is a confirmed Boeing employee at manager level.
- Mara lost the Kestrel-owned crate at the Grayline docks when the Burnouts stole it.
- Mara hired Blackjack to find someone to recover the crate.
- Blackjack hired the player characters for the recovery contract.
- Kilmer advocates opening the crate; the team declines.
- The drop location changes to North Side docks, Pier 15.
- Toggle receives the crate delivery with Skittles present as decker support inside a van.
- Toggle and Skittles are Blackjack associates hired or used under a separate handoff contract.
- The player characters hand the crate off to Toggle and Skittles.
- The crate is unloaded via drone system, moved to a jet ski-sized drone craft, and transported across the river.
- Toggle and Skittles return the crate to Mara.
- Boeing takes both Mara and the crate.
- Toggle and Skittles argue and both depart immediately.
- Back at Siberian Wolf, Snacks signals Switch.
- Lone Star is investigating warehouse arson, associated deaths, and possible murder charges.
- The box truck is returned intact.
- Louie is The Chin's contact and a dock worker / shift supervisor.
- Tonya is present as bartender at the Siberian Wolf.
- Switch shares Lone Star background with the team.
- Switch receives +1 Karma.
- Each runner receives +4 Karma.
- Payment is delivered via courier drop.
- A trideo report covers a warehouse fire, six bodies recovered, and an eyewitness identifying a tall black elf.
- The run is completed clean externally.
- The crate condition may raise upstream questions.
- The Chin requests work from Ivan, a Russian Mafia soldier.
- Ivan reports Chinese Triads pressuring smuggling routes in Gray Line District.
- Ivan directs The Chin to monitor Triad activity, report opportunities, interfere when viable, and support Russian Mafia control.
- Kilmer works on establishing a poker game at Siberian Wolf and canvasses gambling dens.
- Switch makes daily attempts to contact Blackjack.
- The team seeks additional work and monitors Triad presence.
- By Day 3, Blackjack gives no response.
- After a further +2 days, Blackjack is missing / non-responsive for 5 days.
- The team remains active in Gray Line District, monitoring Triads, with operational posture ALERT.
