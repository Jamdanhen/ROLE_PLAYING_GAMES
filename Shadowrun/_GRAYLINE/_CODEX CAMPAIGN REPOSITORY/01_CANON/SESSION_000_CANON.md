- A Kestrel Defense Systems tactical retrieval team enters the Wolf aggressively to recover Spectre.
- The Kestrel force consists of one captain and three enforcers in professional tactical gear.
- Tension escalates into a close-quarters firefight shortly after Spectre escapes.
- The Chin, Switch, Kilmer, Hubcap, and the Kestrel team are involved in the incident.
