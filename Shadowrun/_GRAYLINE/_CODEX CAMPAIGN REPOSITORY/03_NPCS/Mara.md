# Mara

Sources:
- 02_SESSIONS/SESSION_002.MD
- Owner clarification, 2026-06-19

## Type
NPC

## Confirmed Facts
- Mara is a confirmed Boeing employee.
- Mara is manager-level in the Grayline docks crate incident.
- Mara was responsible for the crate when it was lost at the Grayline docks.
- The Burnouts stole the crate from the Grayline docks.
- After the crate was stolen, Mara hired Blackjack to find someone to recover it.
- Blackjack hired the player characters to recover the crate.
- The player characters handed the crate off to Toggle and Skittles.
- Toggle and Skittles were Blackjack associates working a separate handoff contract for Blackjack.
- Toggle and Skittles returned the crate to Mara.
- Boeing took both Mara and the crate after the return.
- Graham Voss was Mara's supervisor.
- Graham may or may not have known about the crate situation when it happened.

## Current Role In Story
- Mara is a confirmed Boeing manager-level actor in the crate recovery chain.
- Her loss of the crate and hiring of Blackjack are confirmed facts, not conjecture.
- Mara is part of the reason Boeing later focuses on Graham: Graham knows where the crate went, that the crate went missing, and that the crate belonged to Kestrel.

## Relationships And Links
- Boeing: employer.
- Graham Voss: supervisor.
- Blackjack: hired by Mara to arrange recovery and then hired the player characters.
- Toggle and Skittles: Blackjack associates hired separately for the handoff chain; they received the crate from the player characters and returned it to Mara.
- Kestrel: original crate owner.
- Burnouts: stole the crate at the Grayline docks.
