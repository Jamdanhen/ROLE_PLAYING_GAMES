## Core Summary

Faust is an indirect supernatural pressure actor whose main function is to generate pressure, temptation, leverage, and escalation.

He is not primarily a battlefield threat, conventional villain, or direct killer.

Faust is most dangerous when others do not realize he is involved.

## Operating Method

Faust identifies emotions already present, then amplifies and rewards them.

Useful emotions and vulnerabilities include anger, envy, greed, resentment, fear, ambition, pride, vengeance, rivalry, obsession, influence, leverage, and unresolved grievances.

People remain responsible for their choices. Faust makes destructive choices easier.

## Preferred Outcomes

Faust benefits when:

- Negotiations fail.
- Retaliation occurs.
- Grudges deepen.
- Alliances weaken.
- Suspicion increases.
- Compromise becomes difficult.
- Rivals escalate against one another.

He prefers directed chaos, sustained conflict, emotional investment, difficult choices, and irreversible decisions.

## Grayline Relevance

Grayline is an ideal environment for Faust because it already contains criminal organizations, smuggling routes, competing interests, economic pressure, territorial disputes, and corporate interference.

Faust appears drawn to places where tension already exists.

## Kilmer Connection

- Faust assisted Kilmer's relocation from the Elven lands to Seattle.
- Faust functioned as facilitator, sponsor, connector, and opportunity provider.
