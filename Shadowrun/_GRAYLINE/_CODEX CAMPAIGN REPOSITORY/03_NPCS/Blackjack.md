Sources:

- 02_SESSIONS/SESSION_002.MD

## Type
NPC / fixer

## Confirmed Facts
- Mara, a Boeing manager-level employee, hired Blackjack to find someone to recover the stolen crate.
- Blackjack hired the player characters for the crate recovery job.
- In Session 1 post-run transit discussion, the team considers delivering to Blackjack, confronting Blackjack, seeking alternate buyers, or hiding/stashing the crate.
- Provides Pier 12 on the North Side as the initial crate drop-off location.
- Has not seen the crate and has no additional intel when Switch reports the crate may be higher value.
- Requests a face-to-face meeting about five minutes after the team begins moving toward Pier 12.
- Meets Kilmer at Rusty Spade.
- Increases payment to 4000 nuyen per runner.
- Requires the crate to remain untampered.
- Requires delivery that night and emphasizes timeliness.
- The team later contacts Blackjack for a drop point.
- The player characters handed the crate off to Toggle and Skittles.
- Toggle and Skittles were Blackjack associates working a separate handoff contract for Blackjack.
- Toggle and Skittles returned the crate to Mara.
- Blackjack has no response by Day 3.
- Blackjack is missing / non-responsive for 5 days at current state.
