## One-Line Job

Velvet wants Markus Vail's off-site blackmail archive destroyed or removed, while leaving Vail's club untouched.

## Velvet's Pitch

"Markus Vail got a club. That ain't the problem.

A club is lights, liquor, noise, and fools pretending the night loves them.

What worries me is what he keeps away from the music.

He's got paper on people who don't survive paper.

Pictures. Names. Favors. Receipts. Little ugly truths in little clean boxes.

That archive goes away, Vail becomes a man with a club again.

That archive stays, he becomes a man people are afraid to disappoint."
