============================================================
SMALL STAT BLOCK STANDARD — SHADOWRUN 3E
============================================================

PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
Create fast-reference NPCs for:

- Combat
- Social scenes
- Random encounters
- Backup enemies
- Contacts
- Security forces
- Gang members

Avoid full dossier format unless needed.

============================================================
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
============================================================

KEEP ONLY:
------------------------------------------------------------
- Combat-relevant data
- Role identity
- Initiative
- Key skills
- Weapons
- Armor
- Threat level

REMOVE:
------------------------------------------------------------
- Full gear inventories
- Lifestyle
- Extensive backstory
- Long skill lists
- Full cyberware breakdowns
- Detailed contacts
- Narrative prose

============================================================
RECOMMENDED FORMAT
============================================================

NAME
------------------------------------------------------------
- Name
- Archetype
- Threat level

CORE STATS
------------------------------------------------------------
B Q S C I W
Reaction / Initiative

COMBAT
------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary weapon
- Damage
- Armor
- Combat pool

KEY SKILLS
------------------------------------------------------------
ONLY:
- 3–6 relevant skills

SPECIAL
------------------------------------------------------------
ONLY if important:
- Cyberware
- Magic
- Rigging
- Unique gear

TACTICS
------------------------------------------------------------
1–3 lines maximum

============================================================
SIZE TARGETS
============================================================

MINION FORMAT
------------------------------------------------------------
3–8 lines

STANDARD NPC
------------------------------------------------------------
10–20 lines

ELITE / RECURRING
------------------------------------------------------------
20–35 lines

FULL DOSSIER
------------------------------------------------------------
Only for:
- PCs
- Major NPCs
- Recurring villains
- Deep contacts

============================================================
EXAMPLE — MINIMAL
============================================================

NAME: Grinder
TYPE: Orc Rigger
THREAT: Moderate

B5 Q5 S4 C2 I6 W5
Reaction 8
Init 8+2D6 / Rigged 10+3D6

SKILLS
- Car 6
- Drone Gunnery 5
- Electronics 4
- SMG 4

ARMOR
- Armored Jacket 5/3

WEAPONS
- Ingram Smartgun X (7M BF/FA)

SPECIAL
- VCR-2
- Bulldog Van nearby
- Rotodrone support

TACTICS
- Avoids direct fights
- Uses drones first
- Retreats if cornered
