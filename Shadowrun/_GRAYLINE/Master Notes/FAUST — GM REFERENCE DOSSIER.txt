FAUST — GM REFERENCE DOSSIER
STATUS: ACTIVE RECURRING NPC / SUPERNATURAL PRESSURE ACTOR
PLAYER KNOWLEDGE: LIMITED
GM KNOWLEDGE: INCOMPLETE
THREAT TYPE: INDIRECT
PRIMARY FUNCTION: PRESSURE GENERATOR

============================================================
CORE CONCEPT
============================================================

Faust is not a battlefield threat.

Faust is not a conventional villain.

Faust is not primarily interested in killing people.

Faust operates through:

- influence
- temptation
- opportunity
- leverage
- manipulation
- escalation

Faust is most dangerous when nobody realizes he is involved.

He prefers others to perform actions for him.

============================================================
OBSERVED CHARACTERISTICS
============================================================

Faust appears intelligent, patient, and long-term focused.

He rarely benefits from immediate destruction.

He appears to prefer situations where:

- choices matter
- pressure accumulates
- emotions intensify
- conflicts become self-sustaining

Faust does not seem interested in random chaos.

He appears interested in directed chaos.

============================================================
KNOWN THEMES
============================================================

Faust appears attracted to:

- ambition
- greed
- pride
- vengeance
- rivalry
- obsession
- influence
- leverage
- unresolved grievances

Faust appears less interested in:

- simple violence
- senseless destruction
- indiscriminate killing

Conflict is useful.

Pressure is useful.

Escalation is useful.

Collapse is often less useful than continuation.

============================================================
OPERATING METHOD
============================================================

Faust does not create emotions.

Faust identifies emotions already present.

Examples:

A person already feels:
- anger
- envy
- greed
- resentment
- fear

Faust encourages those emotions.

He amplifies them.

He rewards them.

He validates them.

People remain responsible for their actions.

The choice is still theirs.

The choice simply becomes easier.

============================================================
PRESSURE PROFILE
============================================================

Preferred Outcomes:

- negotiations fail
- retaliation occurs
- grudges deepen
- alliances weaken
- suspicion increases
- compromise becomes difficult
- rivals escalate against one another

Faust appears most successful when:

People stop evaluating consequences.

People begin justifying impulses.

============================================================
LIKELY MOTIVATIONS
============================================================

Current evidence suggests Faust benefits from:

- increasing pressure
- sustained conflict
- emotional investment
- escalating disputes
- difficult choices
- irreversible decisions

Faust appears to prefer:

Conflict that grows.

Rather than:

Conflict that ends.

This environment naturally generates tension.

Faust appears drawn to environments where tension already exists.

Grayline is ideal.

============================================================
TRIAD / TSAR CONFLICT
============================================================

Current conflict is genuine.

Triads:
- expanding influence
- purchasing property
- increasing Grayline presence

Tsar-aligned interests:
- defending established operations
- protecting influence
- resisting encroachment

These pressures exist independently of Faust.

Faust does not create the conflict.

Faust benefits when:

- compromise fails
- escalation succeeds
- each side interprets the other in the worst possible way

Whether this was:

- genuine assistance

============================================================
IMPORTANT GM RULE
============================================================

Do not over-explain Faust.

Do not fully define Faust.

Do not fully classify Faust.

Do not fully explain origins, limits, or nature.

Faust functions best when:

- partially understood
- partially observed
- difficult to prove
- difficult to explain

The players should understand consequences before causes.

CURRENT WORKING SUMMARY
============================================================

Faust is best understood as:

A catalyst.

A manipulator of pressure.

A facilitator of bad decisions.

A force that identifies existing cracks and encourages them to widen.
