GRAYLINE DISTRICT
TACOMA INDUSTRIAL SECTOR
CANON DISTRICT DESCRIPTION

============================================================
OVERVIEW
============================================================

Grayline sits along the Duwamish River in Tacoma's industrial
district.

The river divides the district into two distinct zones:

WEST BANK
- Burnout territory
- decaying industrial infrastructure
- abandoned facilities
- low oversight

EAST BANK
- active dock operations
- Grayline rail spur
- industrial businesses
- Brigata 12 influence
- Siberian Wolf neighborhood

Grayline is not prosperous.

Grayline is not dead.

It survives through cargo, labor, smuggling, repair work,
and the people who refuse to leave.

============================================================
WEST BANK
============================================================

GENERAL CHARACTER
------------------------------------------------------------

The west bank is the older, declining side of Grayline.

Large portions of the district have fallen into disrepair.

Utility reliability is poor.

Many structures sit partially abandoned.

Street lighting is inconsistent.

The area is heavily influenced by Burnout gang presence.

FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------

- Burnout territory
- burned warehouse (crate recovery site)
- abandoned industrial buildings
- scrap lots
- vacant loading yards
- deteriorated road network
- scattered squat locations

NORTHERN COMMERCIAL STRIP
------------------------------------------------------------

Located along the northern dockfront area.

Surviving businesses include:

- Stuffer Shack
- pawn shop
- liquor store
- small service businesses

These locations serve:

- dock workers
- truckers
- Burnout members
- bridge traffic
- local residents

WEST SIDE DOCKS
------------------------------------------------------------

Smaller dock operations remain active.

Limited freight movement.

Some independent shipping activity.

============================================================
DUWAMISH RIVER
============================================================

Wide industrial waterway.

Primary defining feature of Grayline.

Supports cargo traffic.

Visually separates the west and east bank districts.

PRIMARY CROSSING
------------------------------------------------------------

Bridge crossing.

Major choke point.

Connects both sides of Grayline.

Natural observation point.

Natural smuggling route.

Natural territorial boundary.

============================================================
WOLF BLOCK
============================================================

POSITION
------------------------------------------------------------

One block inland from the east-bank docks.

Separated from the waterfront by:

- industrial business fronts
- alleys
- service access routes
- street network

GENERAL CHARACTER
------------------------------------------------------------

Working neighborhood block.

Mixed-use industrial structures.

Predominantly two-story buildings.

Ground floors:
- businesses
- workshops
- storage
- offices

Upper floors:
- apartments
- lofts
- temporary housing

COMMON OCCUPANTS
------------------------------------------------------------

- mechanics
- dock workers
- truck drivers
- freight brokers
- electricians
- small smugglers
- independent contractors

============================================================
SOUTHERN WATERFRONT
============================================================

Traveling south along the east-bank docks:

1. Cargo Pier
2. Container Maze

============================================================
CARGO PIER
============================================================

Large waterfront cargo facility.

FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------

- cargo staging areas
- warehouse structures
- loading zones
- dock frontage
- freight access roads

Acts as transition point between
main Grayline dock operations and
the southern container district.

============================================================
CONTAINER MAZE
============================================================

POSITION
------------------------------------------------------------

South of Cargo Pier.

Located directly along the waterfront.

GENERAL CHARACTER
------------------------------------------------------------

Massive container storage district.

Dense shipping container stacks.

Narrow corridors.

Limited sight lines.

Difficult navigation.

FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------

- stacked containers
- hidden pathways
- service roads
- blind corners
- storage areas
- smuggling opportunities
- concealed meeting locations

LOCAL KNOWLEDGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Locals know routes.

Outsiders get lost.

============================================================
BRIGATA 12 TERRITORIAL LIMIT
============================================================

The beginning of the Container Maze marks the southern edge
of established Brigata 12 territory.

North of this line:
- regular presence
- active influence
- predictable response

South of this line:
- weaker control
- less oversight
- greater uncertainty

============================================================
DISTRICT IDENTITY
============================================================

Grayline exists between two worlds.

The west bank is what industry leaves behind.

The east bank is what industry still uses.

The Wolf is where people gather.

The docks are where people work.

The Container Maze is where things disappear.
