# Session 007 Useful Guide

Status:
TABLE-READY DEVELOPMENT GUIDE

## Core Use

Run Session 7 as a pressure crossroads.

Start with the office fallout after poker night. Resolve Mouse and Ellis enough to create consequences, then hand the players the drive/Kasumi/Velvet/Grayline options and let them choose the main lane.

Success is not solving everything. Success is leaving the table with one chosen direction and several living clocks.

## Cold Open

Time:
Thursday, June 2, 2057, approximately 12:30-1:00 AM.

Place:
Siberian Wolf office / private room.

Image:
The bar is mostly empty. Chips are being counted. Smoke, liquor, sweat, and tension are still hanging in the air. Frost and Tiny control the threshold. Mouse is calm. Ellis is contained and angry. The Chin owns the room, but every person in it represents someone outside the room.

Opening facts:

- Mouse is cooperative, not submissive.
- Ellis is contained and refusing cooperation.
- Nails has exposed Ellis as not freight.
- Skittles has recoverable drive findings.
- Velvet has flagged a job for The Chin tomorrow.
- The Kasumi Star lead is active.

## Locked GM Boundaries

Do not reveal:

- Full MURMURATION / AI truth.
- Full Boeing chain of command.
- Faust's role in the Kasumi Star arc.
- The artifact's spiritual-countermeasure function.
- That the artifact weakens Faust's influence.
- Full Triad receiving network.
- Velvet's full job details before the in-person meeting.
- That SPLINTER SAINT is Spectre unless discovered.
- Spectre's full motive.

Correct facts:

- `705193` is the lock/access code for the original stolen crate.
- The crate contained the AI tactical computer interface and drone swarm.
- SPLINTER SAINT is the handle Spectre used to lure the Burnouts.
- Spectre did not want the AI loose, but his full motive remains undefined.

## Beat 1 - Mouse

Purpose:
Make Mouse useful without making him friendly.

Mouse truths:

- He is Triad.
- He was watching The Chin, Kilmer, Ellis, and the room.
- He does not work with Ellis.
- He came because Grayline is changing.
- He recognizes the Wolf as more than a bar.
- He knows cargo moving through Grayline will matter soon.

Mouse can say:

- "You think I came here for cards?"
- "That man is not freight."
- "I watched him. I watched you. I watched the room."
- "Grayline is not quiet anymore."
- "You have a bar. You also have a border."

If The Chin calls the Tsar:

- Do not kill Mouse.
- Do not make a show.
- Do not humiliate him.
- Take his name.
- Take who he answers to.
- Take what he was watching.
- Let him leave as an envelope.

Message sent back:

- The Wolf saw him.
- The Wolf knew what he was.
- The Wolf chose discipline.
- Triads with business in Grayline can speak like professionals.
- Watchers sent into Wolf rooms will be watched back.

## Beat 2 - Ellis

Purpose:
Expose corporate observation without solving Boeing.

Ellis truths:

- He is not freight.
- He is a Boeing information probe.
- His cousin story is a cover.
- He wants Graham's status and transfer chain.
- He wants to know whether Ares and Bourgeoisie were involved.
- He is dangerous because of what he reports, not because he can win a fight.

Cover delivery:

- "I'm looking for Graham."
- "He's family."
- "He introduced me to Mara."
- "I was trying to reach her, then she stopped answering."
- "Graham supervises her, so I tried him. He stopped answering too."
- "I went to his office. They said he was supposed to be at work."
- "Someone there said a dark elf had been seen around his apartment, and that the elf was known to come down here."

Cover fracture:

A real cousin asks:
"Is Graham alive?"

A Boeing probe eventually asks:
"Who has him?"

Bait:
"Give me the truth, and I can tell Boeing whatever you want them to hear."

Truth:
He will report what he learns. He is not giving the runners control over Boeing's understanding.

## Beat 3 - Skittles Drive Handoff

Player-facing truths:

- SPLINTER SAINT is a recovered handle, not a known person.

Skittles lines:

## Session Lanes

### Lane A - Boeing / Drive / Original Crate

Questions:

- What did the drive contact?
- What did Cradle ACK mean?
- What did Boeing learn?
- What can `705193` reveal about the original crate?
- What will Ellis report?

Moves:

- Pressure Ellis.
- Ask Skittles for offline analysis.
- Watch for Boeing surveillance.
- Use Ellis as bait, source, or liability.

### Lane B - SPLINTER SAINT

Questions:

- Who used the handle?
- Why lure the Burnouts?
- Was the leak sabotage, theft, containment, exposure, or profit?
- How much can be learned without revealing Spectre?

Moves:

- Investigate the recovered handle.
- Ask Burnout remnants who spread the post.
- Ask Toggle / Blackjack contacts about the board.
- Compare the handle to Spectre behavior later, if earned.

### Lane C - Velvet / Marcellus Vale

Current best rival:
Marcellus Vale, former club promoter turned influence broker.

Public face:
Entertainment manager / talent booker.

Real function:
Keeps leverage on musicians, dancers, fixers, cops, and club owners.

Velvet line:
"Marcellus don't steal money first. He steals the room. Then the money walks over by itself."

Job core:
Destroy or steal physical leverage records: contracts, BTL preference records, backroom recordings, payoff notes, private guest ledgers, photos, names of protected people.

Do not reveal full job details until Velvet meets The Chin in person.

### Lane D - Kasumi Star

Surface:

- MV Kasumi Star inbound.
- Arrival window June 3-9, 2057.
- Grayline Docks.
- Container `KSU-88471-C`.
- Manifest: Chinese Cultural Exhibition Materials.
- Records altered.
- Destination unclear.
- Multiple interested parties.
- Triad-linked interest.

GM truth:
Hidden among the museum cargo is a portable ancient Chinese religious artifact. It is a spiritual countermeasure when ritually activated. The players do not need to learn this.

Player-facing question:
Why did three different parties want the same cargo?

### Lane E - Grayline / Triad / Brigada Diplomacy

Questions:

- Does The Chin tell Ivan/Tsar everything?
- Does Rezak enter as The Chin's Brigada 12 contact?
- Does Mouse become a cautious contact line?
- Is Grayline becoming contested territory?

Rezak:
Brigada 12 leader. Former Russian military. Served with The Chin. Clean violence, no speeches, no wasted motion.

Advances if Ellis is harmed/held, the drive goes live, Boeing is exposed publicly, or Braddock conflict escalates.

Triad:
Advances if Mouse is harmed, The Chin reports him, the runners interfere with Kasumi Star, or the Russians move visibly.

Velvet:
Advances if the runners delay, accept, warn Marcellus, make the job loud, or inspect records before destroying them.

Kasumi Star:
Advances if ignored, investigated publicly, sold as information, or if Brigada/Triads are pulled in.

SPLINTER SAINT:
Advances if the runners ask around, pressure Burnouts, Skittles probes the board, or Spectre notices interest.
