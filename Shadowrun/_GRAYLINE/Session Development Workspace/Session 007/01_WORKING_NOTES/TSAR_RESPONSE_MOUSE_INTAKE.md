# Tsar Response - The Chin Calls About Mouse

Status:
SESSION 007 DEVELOPMENT INTAKE

## Situation

The Chin calls the Tsar about Mouse, the Triad lieutenant currently in the Wolf office.

## Tsar Read

The Tsar listens without interrupting.

When The Chin finishes, he does not sound surprised.

## Delivery Text

"A Triad man does not sit in your bar by accident.

Do not kill him.

Do not make a show.

Do not make him small in front of people who will repeat it.

Take his name.

Take who he answers to.

Take what he was watching.

Then let him leave.

But understand this:

He is not a guest.

He is not a prisoner.

He is an envelope.

Send him back with a message.

The message is this:

The Wolf saw him.

The Wolf knew what he was.

The Wolf chose discipline.

If the Triads have business in Grayline, they can speak like professionals.

If they send watchers into our rooms, those watchers will be watched back."

## Table Function

- Gives The Chin a disciplined response path.
- Prevents the Mouse scene from becoming a public humiliation or killing.
- Frames Mouse as a message carrier rather than a captive.
- Establishes the Wolf/Tsar line: professional contact is acceptable, covert watchers will be answered.
- Lets the runners de-escalate without looking weak.

## Required Information To Take From Mouse

- His name.
- Who he answers to.
- What he was watching.

## Message Sent Back Through Mouse

- The Wolf saw him.
- The Wolf knew what he was.
- The Wolf chose discipline.
- Triads can speak like professionals if they have business in Grayline.
- Watchers sent into Wolf rooms will be watched back.
